# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want QR Quake to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, background, identity, experience level, language or ability. We pledge to act and interact in ways that keep the community open, respectful and healthy.

## What we expect

Examples of behavior that helps the community:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Respecting different opinions, skills and ways of working.
- Giving feedback that is specific and useful, and accepting it with grace.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people who use it.

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- Insults, put-downs, personal attacks or harassment of any kind.
- Sexualized language or images, and unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption and bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any place where someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for explaining and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may warn, temporarily limit or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they judge to be harmful. They will explain the reason when they do.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately. The easiest way is to message the repository owner through the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence, and the person who reports will not face retaliation.
